## Declining a Custom Uptime Guarantee

Dear Marianne,

Thank you for your note of 14 August, and for setting out the reasoning behind the 99.99% availability commitment your operations team has proposed. I want to give you a clear answer rather than a slow one, so I will be direct: we are not in a position to offer a custom uptime guarantee, and I do not expect that to change in the near term.

The reason is not reluctance to stand behind our service. It is that a contractual guarantee above our published tier would commit us to architectural work and third-party dependencies we do not currently control end to end. Offering a number we could not consistently defend would serve neither of us, and I would rather decline than sign something that turns into a quarterly argument about credits.

What remains on the table is what you already have: the availability terms and remedies in Schedule B of our existing agreement, which continue unchanged. Those terms remain in force through the current renewal period.

I recognise this is not the answer you were hoping for, and that it may affect how your team plans around us. If it would help, I am glad to walk your operations lead through our actual availability history and our incident response process, so that any internal assessment rests on real figures rather than a contractual number. I can make time for that call in the next two weeks.

Thank you for raising it plainly. Nine years in, I would rather tell you where our limits are than discover them together later.

Best regards,

James Okonkwo
Head of Customer Operations
