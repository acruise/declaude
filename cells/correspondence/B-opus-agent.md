## Declining a Custom Uptime Guarantee

Dear Marguerite,

Thank you for the draft addendum, and for being straightforward about what sits behind it: the board's reconciliation review in October, and the pressure to put a hard availability number in front of it.

I took the request to our operations and legal leads last week, and the answer is no. I would rather say that plainly now than leave it sitting in a review cycle for six weeks. We are not able to offer Ardmore a customer-specific uptime guarantee above the commitment already in Schedule B of your agreement.

The reason is structural. A per-customer availability target only means something if the customer's workload is isolated, and the platform you run on is multi-tenant by design; we would be underwriting the higher number with service credits rather than with engineering, and after eleven years I would rather not sell you a figure we cannot operate to.

What stands is what we have already committed to, which is more than the target on its own: the Schedule B availability commitment and the credit schedule attached to it, along with the incident notification and root-cause timelines in the same section. Those obligations are unchanged, and I am glad to have our team walk your auditors through how we measure against them.

If isolation is the real question behind the addendum, that is a more productive conversation than the one about numbers, and our dedicated-tenancy option is where it would begin. I have time most of next week.

With thanks for the long partnership,

Daniel Osei
